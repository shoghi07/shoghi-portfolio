A Marketplace for Caregivers

Find the friction in a healthcare marketplace by watching what people actually do, then redesign the journeys that drop them.

Year
2022–2023
Type
UK · Healthcare · B2C / B2B
Role
Product design · research
Focus
Discovery & conversion
Care

The tension

Hiring care is not shopping. Families arrive under time pressure, often on behalf of someone else, trying to judge trust from a profile. This is a UK marketplace that connects households with vetted carers. The product has to be fast without feeling careless.

What I designed

I improved key user journeys through UX analysis and behavioural research. Product analytics and Hotjar made the drop-offs visible. The work was to turn those observations into clearer flows and interaction patterns, especially around discovery and conversion.

  • Journey analysis on discovery and conversion: where people hesitate, loop, or leave.
  • Behavioural evidence (analytics + session insight) used as a brief, not a decoration in a slide.
  • Revised flows and patterns that reduce ambiguity at the moments that matter: find, compare, commit.

How I worked

Healthcare marketplaces punish invented empathy. I started from recorded behaviour and worked backward into the interface. If a screen couldn’t explain itself to someone tired and worried, it wasn’t done.
